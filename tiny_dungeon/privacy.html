Tiny Dungeon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiny Dungeon ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play the Game on iOS or Android. The Game is developed by Emir Orkcu.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Your game progress (character level, gold, soul, equipment, spells, unlocked floors, daily contract progress and settings such as music and sound volume) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, and it is removed when you delete the Game.

2. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • an ad when the Game starts,
  • full-screen ads at natural breaks, for example after you defeat a boss and take the stairs to the next floor, when you claim the daily contract, and after some runs,
  • optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example to revive or to double your gold.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, which ads were shown, clicks and crashes).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

On iOS, the Game may ask for your permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options.

You can learn more about how Google uses data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

3. In-app purchases

You can buy optional items with real money, such as removing ads, a starter pack, and gold or soul packs. Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store your payment details. The Game only receives confirmation of what you purchased and keeps this on your device. If you purchase "Remove Ads", the Game stops showing ads to you.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data security and retention

Because we do not operate servers and do not collect personal information, we do not store any of your data. Information processed by Google AdMob, Apple or Google Play is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove your locally stored game progress, use Settings → Reset all progress or delete the Game from your device. For data processed by Google, Apple or Google Play, please use the tools and contact options those companies provide.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: emirorkcu@gmail.com
