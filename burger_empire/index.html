About

Raise cows, grow feed, run the factory and serve hungry customers. Balance the whole supply chain, launch campaigns, hire managers and grow from one restaurant into an empire.

Available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.

Hakkında

İnek yetiştir, yem üret, fabrikayı çalıştır ve aç müşterilere servis yap. Tüm tedarik zincirini dengele, kampanyalar başlat, yöneticiler işe al ve tek bir restorandan imparatorluğa büyü.

Türkçe, İngilizce, Almanca, İspanyolca, Fransızca, İtalyanca ve Portekizce oynanabilir.

Support

Found a bug or have a question about a purchase? Write to emirorkcu@gmail.com and include your device model and system version.

Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Purchases can be restored under Settings → Restore Purchases, and ad choices changed under Settings → Privacy & Ad Preferences.
